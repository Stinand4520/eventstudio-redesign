Privacy

Last updated November 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to the information you give us on eventstudio.tv. The site is run by Eventstudio Ltd (EventStudio), Oakville, Ontario, Canada.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this site we collect the details you type: your name, your email address and, if you choose to add it, your company. We also see which page the form was sent from. If you call or email us, we keep whatever you tell us.

How we use it

We use it to reply to your enquiry and to quote or plan your event. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to a mailing list because you sent a form.

Who handles it

Form submissions are passed through an email delivery service and arrive in our inbox at admin@eventstudio.tv. The site is hosted on Vercel. These providers process the data only to deliver it to us or run the site.

Site statistics

We measure visits and performance with Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights, and we count things like clicks on our phone number and form submissions so we know which pages are working. Our own scripts may keep a short identifier in your browser (local or session storage) to link an enquiry to the page or ad that led to it. Advertising platforms we use may also measure results through their own tools.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to do the work and keep business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing admin@eventstudio.tv. You can also clear your browser storage at any time.
