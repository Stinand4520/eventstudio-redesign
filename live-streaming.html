How it works

Three things every Virtually Real broadcast has that a plain video call doesn't.

01 — Direction

A director calling shots

Common questions

What's the difference between this and a Zoom webinar?

A webinar platform gives you a screen-share and a call. Virtually Real adds a director, a stage manager, and produced graphics — the difference between a meeting and a broadcast.

Can you direct a stream remotely, without being on-site?

Yes — remote show-calling is one of the core uses of Virtually Real, for events where an on-site crew isn't practical or necessary.
