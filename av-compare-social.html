Case study  ·  Downtown Toronto hotel

I put the kettle on and read it again. It hadn't improved.

The quote

A downtown Toronto hotel sent over its in-house AV quote. The equipment list looked ordinary: four TVs, three wireless microphones, four speakers, some uplighting and a podium. The total was $18,300 before tax.

The catch

The gear was not the expensive part. Service charges, labour and house fees sat underneath the equipment lines and made up most of the gap. The TVs were innocent.

The comparison

We took the same equipment list to independent AV houses and priced it again. The result came in at $10,000 before tax, including our $500 fee. That is $8,300 less, or about 45%, for four TVs, three wireless mics, four speakers, uplighting and a podium in downtown Toronto.

What to take from it

Read your quote from the bottom up. Ask what the service charges, labour and house fees are made of, then get the same equipment list priced by someone else. The price changes when a different supplier quotes it.

Questions planners ask

Is the $10,000 a promise for my event?

No. It is one real comparison on one event, anonymised. Your number depends on your room, your agenda and your date. That is what the comparison is for.

Do you take a commission from the AV houses?

No. You pay us a flat fee and we receive nothing from the AV house you choose.

Who do I sign with?

The AV house you pick. You contract with them directly and they bill you directly.

Will the quotes be like for like?

That is the point of writing one spec first. Every house prices the same list, and we mark which items your agenda requires and which are optional.

My hotel only lets me use its own AV. Can this still help?

Some venues have their own rules for outside suppliers. Check yours before you commit, and send us the hotel quote either way. Seeing the numbers side by side is useful even if you stay in-house.

What do I need to send?

Event date, venue, how many people, and a short agenda. A quote you have already received helps a lot, but you do not need one.
