Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This page explains what information EventStudio (Eventstudio Ltd, Oakville, Ontario) collects through eventstudio.tv and our service pages, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain on purpose.

Information you give us

When you fill in a form or email us, for example to request an AV Compare spec, we collect what you type: your name, email, organization, event details and anything you add in the message. We use it to reply to you and to prepare the work you asked for. We do not sell it or share it for others' marketing.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, we and our service providers may record technical information such as the pages you view, how far you scroll, which buttons you click, your browser and device type, the website you came from, and the campaign link you used to reach us. We use this to see which pages and ads are working and to improve the site.

Cookies and advertising technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (small files and scripts) for site analytics and for advertising. Some of our advertising is run through Chameleon Digital Media, a Toronto advertising company. A tracking script from Chameleon on our pages lets them:

  • show our ads again to people who visited our site (often called retargeting), and
  • count when an ad leads to an action on our site, such as submitting a request form (conversion tracking).

This technology uses cookies and identifiers stored in your browser. It is not used to read your name or email from your device. Chameleon's own handling of information is described in its privacy policy, and you can contact them at admin@ChameleonDigitalMedia.com.

Facebook and Instagram advertising. Our site uses the Meta Pixel, a script from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It tells Meta when someone visits our pages or sends a request form, so we can measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads work and show our ads to people who may be interested. Meta receives information such as the page visited and an identifier from your browser. We do not send Meta the name or email you type into our forms. You can limit this through the switch below, your browser's cookie settings, your ad preferences in Facebook and Instagram, or youradchoices.ca.

Your choices

Advertising cookies on this site. You can turn advertising and retargeting (Chameleon and Meta) off for this browser at any time:

The choice is saved in this browser only and applies from your next page view. If you clear your browser data or switch browsers or devices, you will need to set it again.

  • Browser settings. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Some parts of a site may work less well if you do.
  • Ad choices. You can also limit interest-based ads across many sites through the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada at youradchoices.ca.
  • Email. Every marketing email we send has an unsubscribe link, or you can reply "unsubscribe".

Access, correction and removal

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it removed. Write to admin@eventstudio.tv and we will respond promptly.

Where information is kept

We use third-party tools for email, forms and analytics, and some of them store information outside Canada. We choose providers with care and share only what is needed.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
