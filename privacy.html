Privacy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who we are

EventStudio (Eventstudio Ltd), Oakville, Ontario. Questions about privacy go to admin@eventstudio.tv.

What we collect, and why

When you send a form on this site we collect your name, your email address and, if you choose to add it, your company. We use it only to reply to your enquiry and to quote your event. We don’t sell or rent it.

Services that handle data

  • Vercel hosts the site and provides our analytics and performance measurement.
  • Our email service delivers your form enquiry to our inbox.
  • An advertising partner uses a tracking pixel (px.xfer123.com) on this site to measure visits and may show our ads to people who visited the site.

We also count things like phone-number clicks and form submissions, and our scripts may keep a short identifier in your browser storage to link an enquiry to the page or ad that led to it.

Cookies and ad choices

You can block or clear cookies and site data in your browser’s settings. To opt out of interest-based advertising, visit youradchoices.ca.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years after our last contact, then delete them.

Where data is stored

Some services we use (Vercel, the email service and the advertising partner) may store data outside Canada, including in the United States.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing admin@eventstudio.tv. We’ll reply within 30 days.
